Cookie Policy

What we store in your browser, and why it's kept to a minimum.

Last updated: 12 August 2026
On this page What Are Cookies What We Use Third-Party Resources No Ad Tracking Managing Storage & Cookies Changes to This Policy Contact Us

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit, typically used to remember preferences or track activity. Coginzo's website is intentionally lightweight — this policy explains exactly what we store locally in your browser and what, if anything, third-party resources on our site might set.

01 What We Use

coginzo.com does not set any first-party tracking or advertising cookies. The only data we store in your browser is:

  • Theme preference (local storage) — remembers whether you last viewed the site in light or dark mode. This stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

02 Third-Party Resources

Our pages load a small number of resources from third-party providers to render fonts, icons, and our contact form. These providers may set their own cookies or collect limited technical data (such as IP address) under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — used to load the Space Grotesk, Inter, and JetBrains Mono typefaces.
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs) — used to load icon assets.
  • EmailJS — used to send messages submitted through our contact form directly to our inbox, without storing them on a server we control.

We do not control these third parties' cookie practices; please refer to their respective policies for details.

03 No Ad Tracking

We do not run advertising pixels, retargeting scripts, or cross-site tracking cookies on coginzo.com, and we do not sell browsing data to advertisers.

04 Managing Storage & Cookies

You can clear your theme preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or site data for coginzo.com. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies from third-party domains through their privacy or site-settings menu; doing so will not affect your ability to browse our site, though embedded fonts, icons, or the contact form may be affected.

05 Changes to This Policy

If we introduce analytics or additional third-party tools in the future, we will update this page and, where required, request your consent before doing so. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
